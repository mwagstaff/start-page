Account privacy

You can personalise tab1 without signing in. You can keep your homepage in your browser, connect devices with a QR code, or use an account to recover your settings.

What tab1 saves

Connected devices sync your homepage layout, topics, sources, custom feed addresses, display and search preferences, favourites and read/unread activity. Saved stories may include the headline, link, publisher and image address. App-only topics, offline article files and download preferences stay on your device.

For account sign-in, Google, Apple or GitHub confirms your identity. Tab1 stores a provider-specific identifier to recognise you on other devices. When available, it saves your initial for the account button. Linking providers requires proof of control. Tab1 keeps session credentials and encrypted Apple authorisation information so you can sign out or revoke access when deleting an account.

Storage and deletion

The website server stores connected profiles in a private database. Encrypted daily backups are retained for 30 days. Choose Account in Settings to sign out of a device or delete your account, synced settings and reading activity. Deleting an account also disconnects paired devices. A disconnected device may retain local data until it reconnects or you clear its storage.
